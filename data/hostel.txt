HOSTEL INFORMATION

0. Overview of Hostel Facilities & Residential Amenities
- Campus student hostels provide fully furnished accommodation and amenities including:
  • 2-bed, 3-bed, and 4-bed AC and non-AC residential rooms
  • Study desks, ergonomic chairs, personal wardrobes, and high-speed Wi-Fi
  • Daily 3-meal dining mess (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) with vegetarian and non-vegetarian options
  • 24/7 on-campus maintenance for electrical, plumbing, and room facilities
  • Safe residential environment with biometric gates, 10:00 PM curfew, and round-the-clock security
  • Common study lounges, indoor recreation, and visitor reception rooms

1. Hostel Timings & Curfew Rules
- Hostel gates open at 6:00 AM and close at 10:00 PM every day.
- Curfew is at 10:00 PM; students returning after 10:00 PM require warden sign-in and special entry permission.
- Night-outs and overnight stays outside campus require prior leave approval and parent authorization.

2. Hostel Rooms & Room Amenities
- Rooms are available in 2-bed, 3-bed, and 4-bed configurations with AC and non-AC options.
- Room amenities include study tables, ergonomic chairs, individual wardrobes, beds, and power outlets.
- Room change requests must be officially submitted to and approved by the hostel warden.

3. Mess Timings & Dining Schedule
- Breakfast: 7:00 AM to 9:00 AM
- Lunch: 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM
- Dinner: 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM
- The mess serves hygienic vegetarian, non-vegetarian, and special dietary meals on a rotating weekly menu.

4. Leave & Outing Permissions
- Students must apply for an online leave request through the student portal before leaving campus.
- Weekend leaves and semester breaks require parent confirmation via SMS or portal approval.
- Emergency leaves must be reported directly to the residential warden.

5. Visitors & Guest Policy
- Visitors and parents are permitted only during designated visiting hours (4:00 PM to 7:00 PM on weekdays, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM on weekends).
- All visitors must register with valid photo ID at the hostel reception desk.
- Guests are not permitted inside student residential rooms.

6. Maintenance & Facility Support
- Electrical, plumbing, carpentry, and air conditioning complaints can be registered at the hostel helpdesk or online portal.
- Maintenance issues are typically addressed within 24 hours.

7. Hostel Rules & Code of Conduct
- Maintain silence and respect quiet hours between 10:30 PM and 6:00 AM.
- Smoking, alcohol, tobacco, and prohibited substances are strictly banned on campus with zero tolerance.
- Willful damage to hostel furniture or property incurs replacement penalties.

8. Frequently Asked Questions
- Curfew & Gates: Hostel gates close at 10:00 PM curfew; gates reopen at 6:00 AM.
- Dinner Timings: Dinner is served daily in the mess from 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM.
- Room Changes: Room change applications must be submitted for warden review and administrative approval.
- Visitors: All visitors must register with photo ID at the hostel reception during designated visiting hours.
