ACADEMIC INFORMATION

0. Overview of Academic Regulations, Examinations & Attendance
- University academic standards and student policies include:
  • Minimum Attendance: 75% attendance mandatory in every course to be eligible for semester examinations
  • Internal Examinations: Continuous Assessment Tests (CAT 1 & CAT 2)
  • End-Semester Examination: Final Assessment Test (FAT)
  • 10-Point Grading System: Letter grades S (10 pts), A, B, C, D, E, and F (fail)
  • Course Registration: Online portal course add/drop and prerequisite validation
  • Proctor Mentorship: Assigned faculty mentor for academic guidance and semester planning

1. Attendance Requirements
- Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each registered course to be eligible to write semester examinations.
- Attendance is calculated based on total class sessions attended versus scheduled.
- Students with attendance shortages below 75% require official medical condonation or dean approval according to university regulations.

2. Examinations (CAT & FAT)
- Continuous Assessment Tests (CAT 1 and CAT 2) are conducted mid-semester as internal evaluations.
- Final Assessment Test (FAT) is the mandatory comprehensive end-semester examination.
- Students must adhere strictly to the examination timetable and hall guidelines.
- Carrying a valid physical student ID card and hall ticket is mandatory for all examination sessions.

3. Grading System & GPA Calculation
- Letter grades awarded: S (Outstanding), A (Excellent), B (Very Good), C (Good), D (Average), E (Pass), and F (Fail).
- S grade is the highest academic grade with 10 grade points.
- F grade indicates failure in the course requiring re-registration.
- Semester Grade Point Average (GPA) and Cumulative GPA (CGPA) are calculated based on course credits and grade points earned.

4. Course Registration & Add/Drop
- Students must register for core and elective courses within the designated course registration window.
- Certain advanced courses have prerequisite subject requirements.
- Students must review and verify their final class timetable after the add/drop registration period.

5. Assignments & Continuous Evaluation
- Course assignments and project deliverables must be submitted on or before the announced deadline.
- Late submissions may attract penalty deductions depending on the instructor course policy.
- All submitted work must be original student work adhering to academic honesty.

6. Proctor & Academic Mentorship
- A dedicated faculty proctor is assigned to guide students on academic planning and university regulations.
- Students can schedule meetings with their proctor for course selection guidance and academic support.

7. Academic Integrity & Conduct
- Plagiarism, cheating in exams, and proxy attendance constitute serious academic misconduct.
- Violations are subject to disciplinary action by the academic integrity committee.

8. Frequently Asked Questions
- Minimum Attendance: At least 75% class attendance is required to appear for semester examinations.
- CAT & FAT: CAT 1 & 2 are internal tests; FAT is the final end-semester exam.
- Highest Grade: Grade S is the highest grade with 10 grade points.
