CAMPUS SERVICES INFORMATION

0. Overview of Student Services Available on Campus
- The university provides comprehensive student support services and campus amenities including:
  • Medical Centre & 24/7 Emergency Healthcare
  • High-Speed Wi-Fi & IT Support Helpdesk
  • Free Campus Shuttle Buses & Transit Transportation
  • Sports & Gymnasium Facilities (football, cricket, basketball, badminton)
  • 50+ Student Clubs & Extracurricular Activities
  • Multi-Cuisine Food Courts, Dining Halls & Cafeterias
  • Printing, Photocopying & Reprography Kiosks
  • 24/7 Central Security & Lost and Found Office

1. Medical Centre & Emergency Healthcare
- The campus health centre provides 24/7 basic medical care, outpatient consultations, and first-aid treatment.
- Qualified doctors and nurses are on duty during regular working hours (8:00 AM to 8:00 PM).
- In medical emergencies, students can contact the 24/7 campus emergency helpline or reach campus security for immediate ambulance dispatch.

2. Wi-Fi & IT Support Helpdesk
- High-speed campus Wi-Fi network coverage is available across all academic blocks, libraries, and hostels.
- Students can visit the IT Helpdesk or email itsupport@campus.edu for login credentials, network connectivity, and portal issues.
- Password resets and device registration are handled at the IT service centre.

3. Transportation & Campus Shuttle Buses
- Free campus shuttle buses operate between campus gates, hostels, academic blocks, and the local transit stations from 7:00 AM to 9:30 PM.
- Detailed bus routes and timetable schedules are posted on the student transport portal.
- Students must present a valid student ID card or transport pass when boarding.

4. Sports & Recreational Facilities
- Sports infrastructure includes floodlit football grounds, cricket pitches, outdoor basketball courts, tennis courts, and indoor badminton complexes.
- Gymnasium and indoor sports arenas are open from 6:00 AM to 9:00 AM and 4:30 PM to 8:30 PM.
- Court reservations for badminton and tennis can be booked via the campus sports portal.

5. Student Clubs & Extracurricular Activities
- Over 50+ student clubs spanning technical chapters (coding, robotics, AI), cultural arts, music, dance, and community outreach.
- Annual club recruitment drives take place at the beginning of each semester.

6. Cafeterias & Campus Dining
- Multi-cuisine food courts and cafeterias operate from 8:00 AM to 10:30 PM offering breakfast, snacks, juices, and meals.
- Digital payments, campus smartcards, and UPI are widely accepted.

7. Printing & Photocopying Centres
- Dedicated reprography and printing kiosks are located in the library, student centre, and main academic blocks.
- Services include black-and-white/color printing, scanning, project binding, and photocopying.

8. Lost and Found Desk
- Lost items can be reported to or claimed from the central security office at Main Gate 1.
- Found items must be deposited with campus security within 24 hours.

9. Frequently Asked Questions
- Medical Help: For medical emergencies, call the 24/7 campus emergency helpline or contact campus security for immediate ambulance dispatch.
- Wi-Fi Issues: Contact the IT Helpdesk at itsupport@campus.edu or visit the IT service centre.
- Sports Access: Football, cricket, basketball, tennis courts, and indoor badminton complexes are open to all students.
